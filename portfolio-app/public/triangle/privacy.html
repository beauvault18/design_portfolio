Privacy

Your privacy.

The short version: your assessment answers never leave your device. Beau only receives what you choose to send him through a form, and he never sells or shares it.

Your assessment answers

When you take the Grit Triangle assessment, your answers and scores are calculated in your own browser. They are not sent to Beau or to anyone else.

So the site can show how your triangle changes over time, your scores for each stage (diagnosis, treatment, remission, and so on) are saved in your browser’s local storage, on your device only. To erase them, clear this site’s data in your browser settings. If you use a private or incognito window, nothing is kept after you close it.

The book waitlist

If you join the waitlist, the site collects your email address and nothing else. It is used for one thing: to tell you when The Grit to Heal comes out. Any email you get will include a way to unsubscribe.

The contact form

If you send a message, the site collects your first name, last name, email address, and message. They are used only to read and reply to you.

Please don’t send medical records or anything you wouldn’t want stored in an email inbox. This site is not a medical provider.

Where form messages are stored

Waitlist sign-ups and contact messages are received and stored by Netlify, the company that hosts this website, and forwarded to Beau’s email. Like most web hosts, Netlify also automatically records basic technical information, such as IP addresses and browser type, to keep the site running and secure.

What this site doesn’t do

  • No ads.
  • No tracking scripts, analytics tools, or advertising cookies.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing your information with anyone.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete anything you have sent through a form, or ask to be taken off the waitlist, at any time. Just send a message.

This site is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
