The framework

Three sides. One structure.

Perseverance alone isn’t enough to carry a person through the hardest thing. The Grit Triangle names the three forces that hold a person together when survival is on the line. And like any strong structure, it only stands when all three sides do.

Most of us are taught that grit means gritting your teeth: pushing harder, hiding the cost, refusing to break. But grinding it out is only one side of the shape.

Vulnerability is the day you survive because you let someone see you at your worst. Resilience is the day you fall apart and still return to yourself. Courage is the day you make a terrifying decision and move anyway. Three different things, and healing asks for all three at once.

This isn’t a personality test and it isn’t a diagnosis. It’s a mirror, a way to see which side is holding, which side is quiet, and where you’re being invited to grow next.

“I didn’t get this triangle from a book. I built it from a hospital bed, working backward from what was actually keeping me alive.” Beau Whitman, The Grit to Heal

The three sides

What each side means.

Side One

Vulnerability

The willingness to be seen at your rawest: to ask for help, name your fear, and let people in instead of running from the discomfort of being known.

Where grit begins
Side Two

Resilience

Not being unshaken, but what you do after the shake. Recovering your footing, adapting when plans fall apart, and returning to yourself one steady step at a time.

Passion & perseverance
Side Three

Courage

Moving forward while afraid. Not the absence of fear, but the decision to act anyway: to speak up, try again, and step into what’s uncertain with your knees shaking.

Fear, walking

The book

The Grit to Heal.

Grit isn’t grinding through. It’s what happens when vulnerability, resilience, and courage collide.

Treatment ends, and everyone around you exhales and says, “You did it.” Meanwhile you are still holding your breath. They keep calling you strong. You don’t feel strong. You feel changed, exhausted, and unsure who you are now.

The Grit to Heal is a story and a workbook. The Grit Triangle is a way to see exactly where you’re standing: vulnerability is what allows you to ask for support, resilience is what keeps you getting up, and courage is what lets you face uncertainty and still move forward. Like any strong structure, it only stands when all three sides hold.

This was built in a chemo chair, but it was never really about cancer. If what cracked you open was illness, injury, grief, or any long hard thing, the triangle is yours too.

Who made this.
