Before you write

This is a contact form, not a crisis line, and Beau is a companion, not a clinician. If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself, please reach out to someone you trust or a professional right now. In the U.S., call or text 988 any time.

Replies

Beau answers personally, so replies can take a few days. If you don’t hear back within a couple of weeks, send it again. Things get lost.
