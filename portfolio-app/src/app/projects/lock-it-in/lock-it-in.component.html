What was the problem?

The FIFA World Cup 2026 introduces a historic new format: 48 teams, 12 groups of four, and 104 matches across three countries. Existing sports betting apps are built for standard tournament formats and do not account for this expanded structure.

Casual bettors face a steep learning curve: new group dynamics, unfamiliar qualifying teams, and complex bracket paths that traditional apps fail to visualize clearly. There was no app purpose built for the 2026 World Cup experience.

The opportunity: build a mobile first companion app that makes World Cup betting accessible, social, and easy to follow for fans of all experience levels.

The Solution: A Complete Betting Companion

Lock It In is organized around four core experiences that cover every aspect of World Cup betting:

LIVE ODDS

Real Time Tracking

Live odds integration showing moneyline, spread, over/under, and prop bets for every match. Updates in real time so users never miss a line movement.

BRACKET

48 Team Visualization

Interactive bracket system designed for the new 48 team format. Users can tap through group stages, round of 32, and knockout rounds with smooth animations.

SOCIAL

Group Betting

Create or join betting groups with friends. Shared leaderboards, bet comparisons, and group chat make the World Cup a collective experience.

Eight Bet Types, One Interface

Lock It In supports the full range of sports betting markets, all presented through a clean and intuitive interface:

MONEYLINE

Pick the outright winner of any match. The simplest bet type and the entry point for casual bettors.

SPREAD

Bet on the margin of victory. Goal handicaps level the playing field between favorites and underdogs.

OVER / UNDER

Predict whether the total goals scored will be over or under a set number. Available for full match and halves.

PROP BETS

Player specific and event specific props: first goal scorer, cards, corners, and tournament level futures like Golden Boot.

PARLAYS

Combine multiple bets into a single wager for higher payouts. Visual parlay builder with running odds calculation.

FUTURES

Long term bets on tournament outcomes: group winners, bracket predictions, and championship winner. Updated daily.

LIVE IN GAME

Bet during active matches with dynamically adjusting odds. Next goal, match result, and minute specific props.

GROUP STAGE

Bet on group outcomes: who advances, group winners, and total points. Tailored to the new 12 group format.

UX Design Decisions

  • Bottom Tab Navigation

    Five primary tabs: Home, Matches, Bracket, My Bets, and Social. One thumb reach to any major feature. Designed for fast switching during live matches when every second counts.

  • Match Cards

    Each match displayed as a card showing team flags, current odds, kickoff time, and venue. Cards expand to reveal full betting markets without navigating away from the feed.

  • Bet Slip as Bottom Sheet

    Persistent bottom sheet shows active bet slip with running total and potential payout. Users can add bets from anywhere in the app without losing context. Swipe to dismiss or expand.

  • Color Coded Bet Status

    Active bets shown in gold, wins in green, losses in red, and pending in white. Instant visual feedback across My Bets screen and notification badges.

Technical Architecture

01
React Native with Expo

Cross platform mobile app targeting iOS and Android from a single TypeScript codebase. Expo managed workflow for fast iteration and over the air updates during the tournament.

02
Firebase Backend

Firestore for real time data sync, Firebase Auth for user accounts, and Cloud Functions for serverless bet processing. Real time listeners keep odds and scores updated instantly.

03
Live Odds Integration

API integration for live odds feeds with WebSocket connections for real time updates. Fallback polling ensures reliability during high traffic match events.

04
Bracket Engine

Custom bracket rendering system built with React Native Reanimated for smooth 60fps animations. Handles the 48 team tournament tree with pinch to zoom and tap to expand interactions.

05
Social Layer

Group betting system with Firestore backed real time leaderboards, bet sharing via deep links, and in app notifications for friend activity and bet outcomes.
